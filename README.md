RevFil is an AI-powered product review analysis platform that evaluates customer reviews from e-commerce websites like Amazon and Flipkart to identify genuine and fake (bot-generated or manipulated) reviews. By applying Sentiment Analysis, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Machine Learning techniques, the system analyzes overall customer opinion, detects review authenticity, and provides buyers with insights on whether a product is trustworthy and worth purchasing. The platform helps users make smarter buying decisions through sentiment summaries, authenticity detection, and review-based recommendations.
